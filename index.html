About Chargewise

Chargewise is a modern EV charging management and cost calculation application designed to help electric vehicle owners better understand, track, and optimize their charging expenses and charging history.

Chargewise allows users to:

  • Calculate charging session costs
  • Track charging efficiency
  • Save and manage multiple EV vehicles
  • Maintain charging history across devices
  • Monitor charging statistics and energy usage
  • Store charging sessions securely online
  • Access charging data through a clean and user-friendly interface

Whether you charge at home, office, or public charging stations, Chargewise helps keep all your charging records organized in one place.

Our Mission

Electric vehicles are the future of mobility, but charging cost tracking and charging analytics are often confusing or unavailable.

Chargewise was created to simplify EV charging management and provide clear insights into:

  • Charging cost
  • Charging duration
  • Energy consumption
  • Charging efficiency
  • Charging habits

Our goal is to make EV ownership more transparent, data-driven, and convenient for everyone.

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

Chargewise may store:

  • User profile information
  • Email address
  • Vehicle details
  • Charging session history
  • Charging statistics
  • Authentication information required for login and synchronization
  • Vehicle images uploaded by users

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for:

  • Providing app functionality
  • Synchronizing data across devices
  • Improving reliability and user experience
  • Maintaining account security

Chargewise does not sell, rent, or intentionally share personal data with third parties.

Authentication

Google Sign-In and email authentication may be used to provide secure account access and data synchronization.

Local Storage

Some data may be stored locally on your device to support offline functionality, improve performance, and provide a seamless user experience.

Data Security

Reasonable security measures are implemented to protect user data and maintain secure access to cloud-synchronized information.

Data Deletion

Users may delete vehicles and charging sessions from within the application. For account deletion requests, please contact the developer using the email provided below.
