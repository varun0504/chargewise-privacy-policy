Chargewise Account Deletion

Last Updated: May 2026

Chargewise allows users to request permanent deletion of their account and associated data.

If you would like your Chargewise account to be deleted, please send an email request using the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to the address below.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your Chargewise account.
Email:
varunsingla05@gmail.com

Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon successful verification and processing of your request, the following information will be permanently deleted:

  • User account profile
  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo
  • Vehicle information
  • Vehicle photos
  • Charging session history
  • Charging statistics and related records

Data Retention

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 30 days.

Certain information may be retained for a limited period if required by applicable law, fraud prevention requirements, security investigations, or legitimate business obligations.
